Analyst III (Data Engineer) · TD Legendary Award Recipient · 06/2022 – Present

Awarded the TD Legendary Award for designing the TDS Market Data Service's first centralized knowledge repository and 5+ data platforms, and for setting the standards used to migrate legacy pipelines to a modern stack (Python, SQL, PySpark) across Global Operations, saving 2+ hours per person every day.

Highlights

  • Market-data ingestion. Python pipeline for real-time fixed-income data from Refinitiv, ICE360 and FINRA, with retrieval monitoring and batched execution across multiple VDI nodes. Runtime dropped from 60 to 10 minutes.
  • Broadridge. End-to-end daily price validation and exception pipeline with vendor fallback logic, auto-save and resume, and a 25% scraping runtime. Saves 40 minutes a day.

  • Equity validation and reconciliation. Standardized exception rules across LON, TOR, SYD and LATE. Moving the source from Bloomberg to Dremio cut annual data costs by 60K.
  • Ticker matching. TF-IDF character n-grams with cosine similarity to normalize noisy tickers into Bloomberg format.
  • Cowen SOI, Murex Recon, London Fixing Recon and more. 17 automation workflows in total, saving over 600 hours a year.
