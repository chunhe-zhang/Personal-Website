        How to read this

        Partial snapshots only contain the holdings rows shown on the official public page, so they can leave out most of a fund’s portfolio. Where a Global X fund holds another Global X fund, the layers are multiplied through to single stocks. Layers without data, and positions that aren’t stocks, stay marked as untraced. Leverage can push exposure above 100% of NAV.
